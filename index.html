Useful answers

Luffa 101

How do I prepare a new luffa?

Soak it in warm water for a few minutes to soften the fibres, then squeeze it out. It will become more flexible with use.

How do I keep it clean?

Rinse thoroughly after each use, squeeze out excess water, and hang it somewhere airy to dry completely.

How often should I replace it?

It depends on the job and care. Replace it when the fibres soften too much, develop an odour, or no longer rinse clean.

Can I compost it?

Yes. Remove any hanging cord, cut the luffa into smaller pieces, and add it to a home compost pile.
